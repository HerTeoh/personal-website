These notes are not a blog. A blog post is finished when it's published. A note here is published when it's useful and keeps growing after that.

Each note carries a stage, an idea borrowed from Maggie Appleton's digital garden:

- 🌱 **Seedling**: a rough idea, written down so I don't lose it.
- 🌿 **Budding**: taking shape, revised a few times.
- 🌳 **Evergreen**: as settled as my thinking gets. Still open to change.

Notes are grouped by theme: ASEAN markets, Operations, Building and Data. Those are the threads running through my work so far, from data pipelines to operations to building a business to markets.

Anything about markets here is personal thinking about process and ideas, not investment advice.
